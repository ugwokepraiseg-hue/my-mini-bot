          Transparency

          Wallet information

          What COINVERA stores

          • Your public wallet address
          • The network it belongs to
          • The date you linked it

          What COINVERA never stores

          • Private keys
          • Passwords, PINs or 2FA codes
          • Recovery phrases (12 or 24 words)
          • Any ability to sign transactions for you

          What an address can and cannot do

          A public address can receive funds and be inspected on a block explorer. It cannot move funds or approve transactions. Sharing it is like sharing an account number, not a password.

          Removing it

          Open Wallet → Disconnect. The address is deleted immediately and your Active balance stays untouched.

          ⚠️ Three rules that protect you
          • A real wallet connection never asks you to type a recovery phrase into a chat or website.
          • Never sign a transaction you did not start yourself.
          • COINVERA will never message you first asking for keys, codes or a “validation” payment.
